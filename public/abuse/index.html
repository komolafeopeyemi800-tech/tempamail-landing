Report Abuse & DMCA

How to report spam, fraud, illegal content or copyright complaints involving Tempamail.org addresses.

Tempamail.org is receive-only, but we know addresses at our domain can still be given to websites for the wrong reasons. If you see misuse, tell us and we will look into it.

Report abuse

Email abuse@tempamail.org if:

  • someone used an address at our domain to sign you up for a service without your consent;
  • an address at our domain was used in fraud, phishing, harassment or another illegal activity;
  • you found illegal content in a message or on this site;
  • you found a security problem in the Service.

Please include:

  1. The full address involved (for example name@tempamail.org)
  2. What happened, with dates and times (and your time zone)
  3. Any message headers or links that show the problem
  4. How we can reach you

Remember that messages are deleted after 24 hours, so report quickly. We may block an address, reject mail or restrict access to stop misuse. Where the law requires it, we will cooperate with law enforcement.

Copyright (DMCA) complaints

We do not publish user content: received mail is private to the people who hold the address and is deleted after 24 hours. If you nonetheless believe that something on tempamail.org infringes your copyright, send a notice to abuse@tempamail.org containing:

  • your name and contact details;
  • a description of the copyrighted work;
  • the exact location (URL) of the material you say infringes it;
  • a statement that you have a good-faith belief the use is not authorised by the owner, its agent or the law;
  • a statement, under penalty of perjury, that the information is accurate and that you are the owner or authorised to act for the owner;
  • your physical or electronic signature.

Security vulnerabilities

If you find a security issue, please email abuse@tempamail.org with the details and give us reasonable time to fix it before you make it public. We will not take action against people who report issues in good faith and avoid harming other users' data.
