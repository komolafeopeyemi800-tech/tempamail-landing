Contact Us

How to contact Tempamail.org for support, privacy requests, press or abuse reports.

We are a small team and read every message. Please use the right address so it reaches the right person.

ForWrite to
General questions, feedback, press, advertising enquiriescontact@tempamail.org
Privacy requests (access or deletion questions, policy questions)contact@tempamail.org with "Privacy request" in the subject
Reporting spam, fraud, illegal content or copyright issuesabuse@tempamail.org (see the abuse page)

Before you write

  • I did not receive an email. Check that you copied the address exactly, wait a few seconds and press Refresh. Many websites delay or block disposable addresses. If a site says the address is not allowed, we cannot change that.
  • I lost a message. Messages are deleted after 24 hours and cannot be recovered.
  • I cannot get into an account I signed up for with a temporary address. We cannot restore addresses or messages. Please contact that website's support team and prove you own the account.
  • Someone is using our domain to bother me. Forward the full message, including headers if you can, to the abuse address.

Response time

We aim to reply within a few business days. Abuse reports are prioritised.

Please do not send sensitive personal information by email. We will never ask you for a password or payment details.
