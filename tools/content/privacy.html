This policy explains what information Tempamail.org ("we", "us") handles when you use https://tempamail.org, why, how long we keep it, and what choices you have. We have tried to keep it plain and specific.

1. Information we process

Email messages sent to temporary addresses

When anyone sends an email to an address at our domain, we store the recipient address, the sender's address and display name, the subject, and the message body (text and HTML). Messages are kept for 24 hours, then hidden and permanently deleted. You can delete all messages for your address earlier with the Delete button. Messages over 2 MB are rejected. We do not read messages except where needed to investigate abuse or to comply with the law.

Important: temporary inboxes have no password. Anyone who knows an exact address can read the messages stored for it. Do not use the service for private or sensitive information.

The address stored in your browser

The address you are using is saved in your browser's local storage (key tempamail.address) so that it does not change when you reload the page. It stays on your device. Clearing your browser data removes it.

Technical and request data

Our site and inbox API are delivered through Cloudflare. Like any web service, Cloudflare and our servers receive technical information with every request, such as your IP address, browser type, requested page and time. We use your IP address only to deliver the site, to enforce rate limits that prevent abuse, and to protect the service. We do not build profiles of visitors and we do not link IP addresses to inbox contents. Cloudflare may keep its own logs under its privacy policy.

What we do not collect

We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not ask for your name, phone number or real email address. We do not currently run analytics tools on this site. If we add one, we will update this policy first.

2. Advertising and cookies

Tempamail.org is supported by advertising. We may display ads provided by Google AdSense and its partners. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites, and to measure ad performance.

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet.
  • You can manage personalised advertising in Google Ads Settings, or learn more at How Google uses information from sites that use its services.
  • You can also opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Where required (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), we will ask for your consent before personalised ads or non-essential cookies are used, and you can change your choice at any time.

Apart from advertising partners, this site does not set its own cookies. Our own functionality uses local storage only, as described above. Your messages are never used for ad targeting.

3. How we use information

  • To receive, show and delete messages for the temporary addresses on our domain
  • To operate, secure and rate-limit the service and prevent spam, fraud and abuse
  • To respond to messages sent to our contact and abuse addresses
  • To show advertising that supports the service
  • To comply with legal obligations

4. Sharing

We do not sell your information. We use service providers who process data for us: Cloudflare (hosting, email delivery, database, network security) and advertising partners such as Google. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users or the service.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to our processing of personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to a data protection authority. Because we do not hold accounts, the only data we can look up is the messages for an exact address you give us, and only for 24 hours. To make a request, contact us at contact@tempamail.org. We may need proof that you control the address. California residents: we do not sell personal information.

7. Children

The service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has provided information to us, contact us and we will delete it.

8. International transfers

Our providers operate globally, so your information may be processed in countries other than your own. Where required, those providers use recognised safeguards for international transfers.

9. Security

Connections to the site use HTTPS. Messages open in a sandboxed frame with scripts disabled and remote content blocked. No system is perfectly secure, and the lack of a password on temporary inboxes means you should not rely on them for anything sensitive.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed. Material changes will be highlighted on the site.
